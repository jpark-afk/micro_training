\doxysubsubsection{Detailed Description}
Resource limits for content filtering on a \doxylink{group__DDSDomainParticipantModule_ga1964274885335d5bcea6855f66b0bfe1}{DDS\+\_\+\+Domain\+Participant} and all of its child entities. 

The resources used for content filtering are all pre-\/allocated and shared by all the child entities of a \doxylink{group__DDSDomainParticipantModule_ga1964274885335d5bcea6855f66b0bfe1}{DDS\+\_\+\+Domain\+Participant}. This includes the filters specified by a \doxylink{group__DDSReaderModule_ga49ce0cab2c1b60ddee4784a1432577a4}{DDS\+\_\+\+Data\+Reader} in its \doxylink{structDDS__ContentFilterQosPolicy}{DDS\+\_\+\+Content\+Filter\+Qos\+Policy} and the filters discovered by a \doxylink{group__DDSWriterModule_gafb74fa54675abe1bfb2090265bf4d116}{DDS\+\_\+\+Data\+Writer} for remote \doxylink{group__DDSReaderModule_ga49ce0cab2c1b60ddee4784a1432577a4}{DDS\+\_\+\+Data\+Reader} entities.
