The maximum size of a reliable send window. 

A reliable Data\+Writer may maintain a send window of samples in-\/flight that have not been acknowledged by its Data\+Readers. The max\+\_\+send\+\_\+window is the maximum number of unacknowledged samples in-\/flight.
