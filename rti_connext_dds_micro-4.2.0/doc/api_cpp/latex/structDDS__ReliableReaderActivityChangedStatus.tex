This status is the reciprocal status to the \doxylink{structDDS__LivelinessChangedStatus}{DDS\+\_\+\+Liveliness\+Changed\+Status} on the reader. It is different than the \doxylink{structDDS__LivelinessLostStatus}{DDS\+\_\+\+Liveliness\+Lost\+Status} on the writer in that the latter informs the writer about its own liveliness; this status informs the writer about the "{}liveliness"{} (activity) of its matched readers. 

All counts in this status will remain at zero for best effort writers.
