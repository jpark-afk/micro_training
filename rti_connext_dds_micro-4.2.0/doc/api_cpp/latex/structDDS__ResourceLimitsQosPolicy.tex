\hypertarget{structDDS__ResourceLimitsQosPolicy_DDSResourceLimitsQosModule_usage}{}\doxysubsubsection{\texorpdfstring{Usage}{Usage}}\label{structDDS__ResourceLimitsQosPolicy_DDSResourceLimitsQosModule_usage}
This policy controls the resources that RTI Connext DDS Micro can use in order to meet the requirements imposed by the application and other QoS settings. 

If \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} objects are communicating samples faster than they are ultimately taken by the \doxylink{classDDSDataReader}{DDSData\+Reader} objects, the middleware will eventually hit against some of the Qo\+S-\/imposed resource limits. Note that this may occur when just a single \doxylink{classDDSDataReader}{DDSData\+Reader} cannot keep up with its corresponding \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer}. The behavior in this case depends on the setting for the \doxylink{group__DDSReliabilityQosModule}{RELIABILITY}. If reliability is \doxylink{group__DDSReliabilityQosModule_ggaf09e82d431c2328186c7fb4b3fa9ad91aae82431f2015239328733f3cb41d780d}{DDS\+\_\+\+BEST\+\_\+\+EFFORT\+\_\+\+RELIABILITY\+\_\+\+QOS} then RTI Connext DDS Micro is allowed to drop samples. If the reliability is \doxylink{group__DDSReliabilityQosModule_ggaf09e82d431c2328186c7fb4b3fa9ad91ae5d04022c6405f9db6406489956d1923}{DDS\+\_\+\+RELIABLE\+\_\+\+RELIABILITY\+\_\+\+QOS}, RTI Connext DDS Micro will block the \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} or discard the sample at the \doxylink{classDDSDataReader}{DDSData\+Reader} in order not to lose existing samples.
