DDS\+\_\+\+RELIABLE\+\_\+\+RELIABILITY\+\_\+\+QOS&Specifies that RTI Connext DDS Micro will attempt to deliver all samples in its history. Missed samples may be retried. In steady state, when no modifications are communicated via the \doxylink{group__DDSWriterModule_gafb74fa54675abe1bfb2090265bf4d116}{DDS\+\_\+\+Data\+Writer}, the middleware guarantees that all samples in the \doxylink{group__DDSWriterModule_gafb74fa54675abe1bfb2090265bf4d116}{DDS\+\_\+\+Data\+Writer} history will eventually be delivered to all the \doxylink{group__DDSReaderModule_ga49ce0cab2c1b60ddee4784a1432577a4}{DDS\+\_\+\+Data\+Reader} objects, subject to timeouts that indicate a loss of communication with a particular \doxylink{group__DDSSubscriberModule_ga2755712602505786a23baef5a42d6782}{DDS\+\_\+\+Subscriber}. 

Outside steady state the \doxylink{group__DDSHistoryQosModule}{HISTORY} and \doxylink{group__DDSResourceLimitsQosModule}{RESOURCE\+\_\+\+LIMITS} policies will determine how samples become part of the history and whether samples can be discarded from it. 

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The RTPS spec defines reliability as 0x03 to comply with RTPS spec. However, RTI Connext Core uses 0x3, as well as others.
