A publisher acts on the behalf of one or several \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} objects that belong to it. When it is informed of a change to the data associated with one of its \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} objects, it decides when it is appropriate to actually send the data-\/update message. In making this decision, it considers any extra information that goes with the data (timestamp, writer, etc.) as well as the QoS of the \doxylink{classDDSPublisher}{DDSPublisher} and the \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer}. 

The following operations may be called even if the \doxylink{classDDSPublisher}{DDSPublisher} is not enabled.
