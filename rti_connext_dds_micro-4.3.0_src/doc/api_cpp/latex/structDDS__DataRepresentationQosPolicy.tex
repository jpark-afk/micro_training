This policy has request-\/offer semantics. A \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} may only offer a single representation. Attempting to put multiple representations in a \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} will result in \doxylink{group__DDSReturnTypesModule_gga73f148aaf96b5f6f9fc630b7cfeb0c91a5128152d6418ba270e44c5de487ce4c0}{DDS\+\_\+\+RETCODE\+\_\+\+INCONSISTENT\+\_\+\+POLICY}. A \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} will use its offered policy to communicate with its matched \doxylink{classDDSDataReader}{DDSData\+Reader} entities. A \doxylink{classDDSDataReader}{DDSData\+Reader} requests one or more representations. If a \doxylink{classDDSDataWriter}{DDSData\+Writer} offers a representation that is contained within the sequence of the \doxylink{classDDSDataReader}{DDSData\+Reader}, the offer satisfies the request and the policies are compatible. Otherwise, they are incompatible.
